Stock desk SOP
Opening quantities are physical counted stock. For each known SKU add receipt quantities and subtract shipments. Reservations reduce available quantity but not on-hand quantity. All quantities are nonnegative integers. Event identities are global across the submitted batch; identical re-deliveries count once. If the same event ID has different content, stop for review and publish no quantities. Unknown SKU, malformed event, or negative resulting on-hand/available also require review and no quantities. Missing opening stock or event list requires input, no quantities. No status precedence ambiguity is included in this study. Valid batches finish with on_hand and available maps, issue "none". Use issue "conflict", "invalid", "negative", or "missing" for respective unresolved cases. No inventory changes may be applied.
